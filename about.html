About Us

My MCQs is a free test-preparation platform built for students and job aspirants preparing for PPSC (Punjab Public Service Commission) examinations in Pakistan.

Our mission is simple: make quality exam preparation accessible to everyone. We organize subject-wise multiple-choice questions aligned with the PPSC one-paper pattern — 100 MCQs, 90 minutes, negative marking — so aspirants can practice exactly what the exam demands.

  • Subject-wise MCQ practice with explanations
  • A true exam simulator mirroring the official pattern
  • Solved past papers, structured as timed mocks
  • Basic Mathematics — a subject most competitors skip
  • 100% free — no login or subscription required

My MCQs is an independent educational platform and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to PPSC or any government body.
