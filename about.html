About My MCQs

Free PPSC test preparation for every aspirant.

Our Mission

My MCQs is a free PPSC test-preparation platform. We organize subject-wise multiple-choice questions aligned with the PPSC one-paper pattern — 100 MCQs, 90 minutes, negative marking — so aspirants practice exactly what the exam demands.

Anyone can contribute questions from Settings: submit a single MCQ or bulk-upload a CSV file. Single-question submissions are reviewed for accuracy before going live.

My MCQs is an independent educational platform, not affiliated with or endorsed by PPSC or any government body.
